Background

The challenge of accessible healthcare in underserved communities

Diagnostic services are often fragmented and time-consuming. Patients struggle with booking tests, tracking reports, and navigating multiple touchpoints.

At the same time, internal teams face unorganized workflows across front desk, admin, and home collection—leading to delays, errors, and poor coordination.

The lack of a unified system impacts both patient experience and operational efficiency, highlighting the need for a complete redesign.

THE PROBLEM

Existing healthcare services are not designed to serve marginalized populations

Hospitals were struggling with outdated processes:

  • The old system was over the place and did not work well.
  • The existing system had a lot of problems.
  • Patients had a time booking things and keeping track of their reports.
  • The people at the desk had to do a lot of work by hand which meant they made mistakes.
  • The people who collected things from homes did not have a plan of what to do.

OUR GOAL

Create a connected, user-friendly system that simplifies operations for all stakeholders.

We want to make a system that's easy for people to use when they need to get diagnosed. This system should make things simple for patients.

The main idea is to make it easy for people to book tests get their reports and have everyone work together smoothly. The people at the desk the administrators and the ones who collect samples from homes. We want to reduce mistakes save time and make the service better, for ecosystem. This will help the diagnostic ecosystem to work better.
